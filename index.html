Question & Answer

FAQs - Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about the app, packages, and support.

How will the app be useful for me ?
+

The app connects you directly with farmers and vendors, letting you order fresh produce, track deliveries, and manage payments — all from one place.

What support will I get in the premium package?
+

Premium includes priority support, onboarding assistance, extended warranty on logistics, and access to exclusive vendor deals.

Can I get updates for free?
+

App updates that improve stability and security are provided free. Major feature upgrades may be part of premium releases.

How to setup account?
+

Create an account using your phone or email, verify using the OTP, then complete your profile to start ordering and selling.

Is there any hidden cost?
+

No hidden costs — fees and charges are displayed clearly at checkout, and subscription details are shown before purchase.

What is the process to get a refund?
+

Open a support ticket or call our helpline within 48 hours of delivery. We investigate and process eligible refunds within 5-7 business days.

Is there any discount on packages?
+

We offer seasonal discounts and bundle pricing; check the offers section in-app or contact sales for custom pricing for large orders.

How to contact support quickly?
+

Use the "Call us now" or "Email us" buttons in the support section, or start a chat inside the app for immediate assistance.
